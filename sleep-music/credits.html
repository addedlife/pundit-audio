Free recordings only (public domain, CC0, or Creative Commons licenses that allow sharing). Each file was loudness-normalized to about −20 LUFS, given a short fade in/out and a 3-second gap, and encoded to 112 kbps MP3. Calm pieces also have a gentle treble cut (about −4 dB above 5 kHz) to keep them warm. Spoken host introductions and “That was…” back-announcements are synthesized (Kokoro voice “bm_fable,” British male, default speed, no pitch or EQ changes).
