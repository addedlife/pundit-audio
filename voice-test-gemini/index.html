I did not use gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts, because that same page marks its free tier as “Not available.” I did not enable billing. Free-tier limits and daily use

Official sources, checked today:

  • Pricing — For gemini-3.8-flash-tts, input and output on the free tier are “Free of charge.” Audio is billed on the paid tier at 25 tokens per second of audio. The free-tier column also says content is “Used to improve our products.”
  • Rate limits — Limits are requests per minute (RPM), tokens per minute (TPM), and requests per day (RPD), applied per project. RPD resets at midnight Pacific time. The official page does not publish the numeric free-tier RPM/TPM/RPD for this TTS model; it says to view your active limits in Google AI Studio. Specified limits “are not guaranteed and actual capacity may vary.”
  • Model page — Input limit 8,192 tokens; output limit 16,384 tokens. At 25 audio tokens per second, that is about 10.9 minutes of audio in one request. I confirmed the 25 tokens/second figure on these samples (for example, Charon’s news clip was 1,435 output tokens and 57.4 seconds).
  • Gemini API Additional Terms (effective March 23, 2026) — On unpaid services, including unpaid Gemini API quota, Google uses the content you submit and the generated responses “to provide, improve, and develop Google products and services and machine learning technologies.” Human reviewers may read, annotate, and process API input and output after it is disconnected from the account and key. The terms say: do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information to the unpaid services. (If the account were in the EEA, Switzerland, or the UK, a different “Paid Services” data section would apply even when the quota is free; this key’s project is being used as a US free-tier key.)

What that means for about four briefs a day: a 12-minute brief (today’s morning test was 12.3 minutes) is longer than the 10.9-minute output cap, so it would need about two requests, not 15–20, if we send long chunks. Four briefs would be about eight requests a day. The music intros can stay pre-recorded, so they would not use daily quota. I cannot officially say that eight requests fits, because Google does not publish the free-tier RPD for this model on the rate-limits page. Unofficial forum posts from November 2026 (not Google documentation) report a free-tier cap of 3 RPM, 10,000 TPM, and 10 requests per day for Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS. If that figure is right, eight requests a day would only just fit, with almost no room for a retry, and 15–20 calls per brief would not fit at all. This comparison itself used 10 successful requests in one session and did not receive a quota error, which is consistent with a daily cap of at least 10, but is not proof of the official number. Please check the project’s live limits in AI Studio before relying on this for daily briefs.

Bottom line: daily use on the free tier is only feasible if each brief is sent in one or two long requests and the project’s actual RPD is at least about 8–10. It is not feasible if the official cap is 10 RPD and we also need retries, or if the brief has to be split into many short calls. I have not switched the live engine to Gemini.
